\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

ADR4AI investigates how to connect industrial requirements to AI-system
architectural patterns and inspectable decision records without treating
model-generated recommendations as established architectural choices.
The approach exhaustively assesses requirement--pattern pairs, retains
requirement evidence for positive classifications, and generates Proposed
ADR drafts linked to those selected pairs. A separate frozen
retrieval/reranking baseline enables a descriptive candidate-overlap comparison.

Within the bounded sample of \StudyStoryCount{} user stories from
\StudySystemCount{} systems and \StudyPatternCount{} patterns, the
\StudyAssessmentCount{} assessments predominantly yielded insufficient
information (\StudyInsufficientInformationCount{} pairs;
\RqOneInsufficientInformationPercent{}\%), with
\StudyApplicableCount{} pairs classified as applicable.
The prevalence of insufficient information limits conclusions about pattern
applicability; it does not establish that the requirements are defective or
that the unresolved patterns are unsuitable.
Only \StudyRagOverlapCount{} of the \StudyApplicableCount{} LLM-positive
pairs (\RqTwoOverlapPercent{}\%) appeared in the same-story final
Top-\RagFinalCount{} retrieval set. This divergence describes candidate
membership, not the superiority of either approach, particularly given the
protocols' differing input languages and catalog representations.
All \StudyApplicableCount{} selected pairs have one structurally complete,
traceable Proposed ADR, establishing generation completion and provenance
for \StudyAdrCount{} records, rather than semantic quality or professional
acceptance.

The prepared expert assessment is intended to complement these computational
findings with item-specific practitioner judgments of the presented pairs and
records. Whether those judgments corroborate, qualify, or challenge the
recommendations remains unknown. They cannot establish missed-pattern recall
or deployment success. The combined study outcome remains unresolved until
the expert evidence is verified and integrated.
